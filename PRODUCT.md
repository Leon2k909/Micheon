# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Leon and Michelle — an English-speaking learner of German and a German native learning alongside — plus friends and family they share the installer with. Used at home on Windows desktops, in relaxed evening sessions, fully offline. Learners range from absolute beginner (A1) to lower-intermediate (B1); the app must never assume classroom vocabulary or grammar jargon.

## Product Purpose

Micheon is an offline-first desktop app for learning real conversational German: full sentences and dialogues over word lists, hand-written content over generated filler. Success means a learner can walk into everyday German situations — the bakery, the Amt, the night train — and recognise what people actually say. Everything ships in the installer: no accounts in the cloud, no paid APIs, no network required to learn.

## Brand Personality

Warm, handcrafted, quietly playful. "Made with love by Leon & Michelle" is literal — the app should feel like a gift between partners, not a product with a funnel. Encouraging without being loud; gamification (streaks, XP, achievements) supports the habit but never nags. The green-and-cream world with the waving monkey mascot and the Michelle companion sets a cosy, storybook tone that stays out of the way during actual practice.

## Anti-references

- Duolingo's pressure mechanics: no guilt streaks, no aggressive push toward daily quotas, no paywalls or gem economies.
- Corporate SaaS chrome: no marketing gradients, no tracked-uppercase badge noise, no dashboard-metric worship.
- Textbook austerity: no grammar-first drilling, no conjugation tables as a landing experience, no jargon ("reflexive", "dative") in learner-facing copy.

## Design Principles

1. **The lesson is the hero.** Navigation, gamification, and updater chrome stay quiet so the sentence being learned owns the screen.
2. **Real German, really said.** Content and UI copy alike prefer what people actually say — including regionalisms and cultural notes — over textbook forms.
3. **Tactile calm.** Soft cream surfaces, one green accent family, gentle depth (soft shadows, rounded corners); motion conveys state in 150–300 ms and never decorates.
4. **Offline is a feature.** Every surface must work with zero network; nothing may degrade into spinners waiting for a service that isn't there.
5. **Two languages, one screen.** German and English appear together wherever a learner might hesitate; hover glosses fill any gap.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard-first practice (every exercise action has a shortcut, documented in-app), visible focus states, aria-live for async state, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured on every animation, WCAG AA contrast on text, and a full dark theme that follows the learner's setting.
